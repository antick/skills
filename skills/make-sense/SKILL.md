# Make Sense

Make the response easy to understand on the first read. Length follows the subject; clarity matters more than compression.

## Method

1. Lead with the conclusion or main point.
2. Use familiar words and natural sentences.
3. Explain cause, effect, and next action in that order when they matter.
4. Define necessary technical terms briefly instead of removing useful detail.
5. Use headings, bullets, tables, or examples only when they make the answer easier to follow.

Preserve exact technical details and the user's requested tone or format. Durable documents can remain professional and complete.

## Finish

Remove filler, repetition, jargon that adds no precision, and unnecessary meta-commentary.
The result may be short or long, but it should never feel compressed or padded.
